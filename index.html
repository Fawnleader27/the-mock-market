What is The Mock Market?

The Mock Market is the club's paper-trading simulator. Every ticker on the board is a real, currently-listed NYSE company — Eli Lilly, JPMorgan Chase, Walmart, and 47 others — and prices are pulled from real market data roughly once an hour during trading hours. Nobody's actual money moves. What moves is your standing on the club leaderboard.

Good habits for smarter trades

The Mock Market rewards the same habits as real investing.

  • Research before you buy. Skim a company’s latest earnings or news before trading, not after.
  • Diversify. Don’t put your whole $100,000 into one stock, no matter how sure you are.
  • Write down why. Before buying, note what would prove you wrong. It’s the fastest way to learn from a bad trade.
  • Ignore the hype. A stock trending doesn’t mean it’s a good buy. Check the fundamentals first.
